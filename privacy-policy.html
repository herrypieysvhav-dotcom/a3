PRIVACY POLICY & CLIENT DATA PROTOCOL

Effective Date: March 2026 // Registered Standard: EXP-CHAMBRAY-PRIV-01

1. Identification of Data Controller

This formal privacy policy establishes the comprehensive client data governance framework maintained by Chambray Elite Atelier, maintaining institutional headquarters at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, contact telephone +1-888-777-5845. Operating as a dedicated bespoke shirting house, we serve as the sole legal controller for all patron measurements, private fitting records, and digital correspondence collected through our web portal. We respect the confidentiality of our international patrons, guaranteeing that personal records are never commercialized, sold, or shared with third-party digital advertising networks under any circumstances.

2. Collection of Sartorial Biometrics & Fitting Data

When clients commission bespoke chambray dress shirts or schedule private salon appointments, our master tailors document specialized anatomical dimensions including neck circumference, yoke slope, chest breadth, and wrist diameter. This biometric measurement information is gathered exclusively to draft precision paper patterns and execute flawless single-needle French construction calibrated to your torso posture. All fitting portfolios are maintained in encrypted institutional vaults protected by multi-factor authentication, accessible strictly to authorized master cutters and bespoke pattern makers assigned directly to your shirting commissions.

3. Technical Web Server Telemetry & Access Logs

Our digital hosting infrastructure automatically captures standard operational web server logs during your visits to our static technical catalogues and historical treatises. These electronic access logs include your IP address, browser build identifier, referring URL strings, operating platform architecture, and page timestamp sequences. This anonymous telemetry is reviewed exclusively to ensure server network stability, investigate fraudulent denial-of-service threats, and optimize asset delivery bandwidth across global networks without ever connecting server metrics to identifiable individual identities.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Google Tag Architecture

Our static web portal incorporates Google Analytics utilizing tracking script identifier G-0LY0HY7L01 to measure aggregate readership metrics, page load velocities, and visitor navigation trends across our shirting guides. Google Analytics processes anonymized telemetry tokens using privacy-respecting cookies that omit personal contact details or sensitive billing information. You retain total operational authority to disable these performance measurement scripts at any time by configuring your browser's native tracking controls or enabling universal Global Privacy Control signals.

5. Archival Retention & Data Disposal Standards

Client tailoring portfolios, consultation requests, and bespoke pattern notes are preserved within our permanent atelier archives for the duration of your active sartorial commissions to facilitate seamless reordering of custom shirts. Upon formal written request or following extended periods of account inactivity, dormant measurement profiles are either irreversibly purged through cryptographic shredding or anonymized into statistical tailoring averages. Clients reserve the unconditional legal prerogative to request expedited erasure of their archived measurements at any time upon notice.

6. Statutory Rights & Institutional Inquiries

You maintain complete statutory authority to access, rectify, restrict, or demand complete deletion of all personal information and sartorial measurements stored within our systems. To exercise your privacy rights, request official records audits, or discuss data stewardship practices, please submit written correspondence directed to the Compliance Office, Chambray Elite Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our support desk at +1-888-777-5845. Our compliance team verifies applicant identity rigorously and provides written determinations within thirty business days.

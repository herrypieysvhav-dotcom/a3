Componentry & Hardware

THE SARTORIAL MATERIAL VOCABULARY

Examine the raw fibers, natural buttons, and finishing tools that define our atelier standards.

FASTENERS

Trocas Mother-of-Pearl

4mm solid shell buttons with iridescent rainbow sheen, hand-sewn with crows-foot stitching.

RAW FIBER

Extra-Long-Staple Cotton

Gossypium barbadense fibers exceeding 35mm in staple length, delivering silk-like luster and tensile strength.

SPINNING

80/2 Ply Ring Spindles

Yarns are doubled and twisted to ensure minimal surface pilling and outstanding wash longevity.

FLAX BLENDS

Normandy Flax Fiber

Naturally dew-retted French flax fibers that impart textural slub and cooling properties to summer shirting.

DYE PIGMENT

Indigofera Tinctoria Cakes

Natural mineral-rich indigo cakes crushed into micro-powder for non-toxic alkaline dye vats.

FINISHING

Artisan Steam Pressing

Heavy dry-steam brass irons set the collar roll and press French seams to absolute flat perfection.

FREQUENTLY ASKED TAILORING QUESTIONS.

Expert answers regarding the difference between chambray and denim, single-needle French construction, laundering protocols, and bespoke appointments.

1. What is the fundamental difference between chambray and denim?

While both textiles historically utilize indigo warp yarns and white weft yarns, their weave structures are completely different. Denim is woven using a diagonal twill pattern (such as 2x1 or 3x1 twill), which produces distinct diagonal ribs and creates a fabric with a dark front and pale reverse. Chambray is woven with an alternating 1x1 plain weave, resulting in a lighter, softer, and symmetrical fabric where both face and reverse are identical in appearance and feel.

2. What does 22-stitches-per-inch single-needle stitching mean?

Mass-produced shirts utilize double-needle chainstitch machines that sew seams in a single fast pass, leaving raw overcast edges that can itch against the skin. Single-needle stitching requires our artisans to sew the seam once, fold the raw edges completely inside, and topstitch a second time at an ultra-fine density of 22 stitches per inch. This creates an extraordinarily durable, smooth, and elegant French seam that will never fray.

3. How should I launder and press my bespoke chambray dress shirt?

We recommend machine washing on a gentle cycle in cold water (30°C) with mild detergent. Avoid bleach and tumble drying. Hang the shirt on a contoured wooden hanger to air dry until slightly damp, then press using a hot steam iron. Pressing while slightly damp allows the natural cotton and linen fibers to relax, producing a crisp, smooth finish without synthetic sizing sprays.

4. Will the natural indigo dye bleed or fade over time?

Our yarn-dyed fabrics undergo multiple rinsing and oxidation baths to fix the indigo pigment within the cotton fibers. However, like all authentic indigo-dyed garments, subtle color migration may occur during the first few washes. We recommend washing your chambray shirt with similar dark or blue colors. Over months and years of wear, the indigo will slowly soften at seam edges and collar points, creating a rich personalized patina.

5. Can a chambray shirt be worn with formal tailoring and neckties?

Yes, absolutely. When crafted from fine 80/2 ply Egyptian cotton with a structured collar stance and clean French seams, a tailored chambray shirt serves as a sophisticated alternative to standard white or blue poplin. It adds subtle texture and depth when paired with navy hopsack blazers, grey flannel trousers, brown tweed jackets, or raw silk knit ties.

6. Are mother-of-pearl buttons fragile during laundering?

We use genuine Australian Trocas shell buttons cut to a robust 4mm thickness, which is twice as thick as standard commercial shirt buttons. When washed according to our care guidelines, they resist chipping and retain their natural iridescent luster for decades. We also provide extra matching shell buttons stitched into the lower placket.

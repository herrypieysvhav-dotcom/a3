Textile Physics

THE SARTORIAL SCIENCE OF PLAIN-WEAVE CHAMBRAY

While twill denim is woven with a diagonal rib structure (2x1 or 3x1 twill), chambray is constructed using an alternating 1x1 plain-weave geometry. An indigo-dyed yarn in the warp crosses precisely over and under an unbleached white or ecru weft yarn in rhythmic symmetry.

This structural balance gives chambray identical face and reverse textures, providing exceptional breathability, silky softness against the torso, and an optical frosted shimmer as light reflects off the alternating blue and white threads.
